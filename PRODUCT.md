# Batch Tracer

## Platform
Web, desktop and tablet first with responsive mobile access.

## Product truth
The governing brief is prd.md v1.1. Independent educational simulation, synthetic data only. Six roles collaborate on batch genealogy, quality disposition, append-only stock movements, shipments and recall reconciliation. All authorization and quantity rules belong on the server.

## Implementation direction
Inferred from the approved PRD and instruction to complete implementation: an operational workspace with persistent navigation, searchable tables, dossier detail pages and explicit transition confirmations. English domain labels match the PRD. No external notifications or evidence uploads in MVP.
